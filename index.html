Syed Ijaz Ul Haq

I am a PhD candidate in Agriculture Engineering (Remote Sensing & Precision Agriculture) at Shandong University of Technology, China, advised by Prof. Yubin Lan. I am affiliated with the School of Agriculture Engineering and Food Sciences. I am also associated with the Department of Agronomy, PMAS-Arid Agriculture University, Rawalpindi, Pakistan.

Research  My research focuses on AI-driven precision agriculture, investigating how machine vision, deep learning, and remote sensing can be applied to improve crop monitoring, weed detection, and precision spraying systems. I have worked with computer vision, agricultural robotics, and multi-dimensional optical remote sensing.

Specifically, I work on weed detection using deep learning (Applied Sciences, 2023), machine vision-based delay compensation for row-following spraying (Agronomy, 2026), and multi-dimensional optical remote sensing for crop stress monitoring (Smart Agricultural Technology, 2025).

Selected Publications

AGRONOMY
Machine Vision Precision Spraying Delay Compensation

Design and Experimental Evaluation of a Machine Vision-Based Delay Compensation Algorithm for Corn Row-Following Spraying

A delay compensation method based on machine vision and dynamic ROI adjustment was proposed for corn row-following operations. The method integrates real-time forward-velocity information from GNSS/IMU, decomposes delays associated with image processing, command transmission, and actuator motion. The algorithm achieved row-following accuracy of 93.75% in bench tests.

Qing-Shuai Sun, Can-Can Song, Yu-Bin Lan, Yanxi Li, Syed Ijaz Ul Haq, Xuejian Zhang, Guo-Bin Wang

Agronomy 2026

Multi-Dimensional Optical Remote Sensing in Agriculture: Spectral, Angular, and Spatial Scaling for Crop Stress Monitoring

SCI REPORTS
Precision Weeding Mechanical Design

Design and Performance Evaluation of a Spiral Bar Precision Weeding Mechanism for Corn Fields

A spiral tendon-type precision weeding device specifically designed for corn fields. The device achieves a weed removal rate exceeding 95% with a 3% wounding rate under optimal conditions.

Wenze Hu, Syed Ijaz Ul Haq, Yubin Lan, Zhihuan Zhao, Shadab Ahmad, A. Al Bahir, Jun-Ke Zhu, Atiku Bran

Scientific Reports 2024

Zinc-Oxide Nanoparticles Ameliorated the Phytotoxic Hazards of Cadmium Toxicity in Maize Plants by Regulating Primary Metabolites and Antioxidants Activity

Green synthesis and application of zinc-oxide nanoparticles to alleviate cadmium stress in maize. Exogenous application of ZnO nanoparticles (25-50 mg/L) significantly alleviated cadmium toxicity.

Mujahid Hussain, Rehana Kaousar, Syed Ijaz Ul Haq, Changfeng Shan, Guo-Bin Wang, Nadia Rafique, Shizhou Wang, Yubin Lan
